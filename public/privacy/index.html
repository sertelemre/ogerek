Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Ogerek (“we”) makes browser extensions and small business tools. This policy covers this website and purchases of Ogerek products. Each product also has its own privacy policy that explains exactly what that product does with your data — for example the SnapTable Privacy Policy.

This website

ogerek.com has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. The site is served by Cloudflare, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser and time of the request) for a short period to keep the service secure and running.

Our products

Our extensions are designed to work locally in your browser. What a product reads, stores or sends is described in its own privacy policy, and we only collect what that product needs to work (for example, a license key to check a subscription).

Payments

Purchases are processed by Creem, our merchant of record. Creem collects your payment details, name, email and billing country under its own privacy policy. We receive order information (such as email, country, product and subscription status) to deliver licenses and provide support. We never see your full card details.

What we don't do

We don't sell personal data, we don't run ads, and we don't share your information except with the service providers needed to run our products (Creem for payments, Cloudflare for hosting) or when the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to hello@ogerek.com. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK) you may have additional rights, which we will honour.

Changes

We will update this page if anything changes and adjust the date above.
