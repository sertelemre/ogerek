Questions? Answers.

How do I activate Pro?

After paying you get a license key by email (it's also in the Creem customer portal). In LocalSession open Settings → Plan & license, paste the key and press Activate.

How isolated is a profile?

Cookies are separated at the network level: requests from a profile tab carry only that profile's cookies. Site storage such as localStorage and IndexedDB is kept apart per profile inside the page. A site's own service worker runs outside any tab, so LocalSession removes those for sites you open in a profile.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes — one Pro license works on up to 3 Chrome profiles. Use “Deactivate on this computer” in Settings to move it. To take a logged-in profile with you, export an encrypted backup.

How do I cancel?

Any time, from the Creem customer portal. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Profiles beyond the free three are locked, never deleted.

Do you see my logins?

No. Profiles and their cookies are stored only in your browser. The only thing LocalSession sends anywhere is the license check for Pro. See the privacy policy.

Is using several accounts allowed?

That depends on each website. Some services limit how many accounts one person may run — you're responsible for following their terms. See our terms.
