Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

SnapTable AI (“SnapTable”, “we”) is a Chrome extension operated by Ogerek. This policy explains what data the extension and this website handle. Short version: the data you scrape stays in your browser.

What stays on your device

  • Page content. When you open the side panel, SnapTable reads the page you are viewing to detect tables and lists. This happens locally; page content and scraped rows are never sent to us.
  • Exports. Excel, CSV, JSON and Markdown files are generated on your computer.
  • Settings and usage counter. Your monthly free-row counter and preferences are stored in Chrome storage. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync, Chrome syncs this small counter between your devices.

What leaves your device

  • License checks (Pro only). When you activate or re-validate a license, the extension sends your license key and a device label (e.g. “SnapTable · MacIntel”) to our license server, which runs on Cloudflare and forwards the request to Creem. Cloudflare may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time) for a short period.
  • Abuse prevention (Pro only). To stop shared or refunded keys from being used, the license server keeps a one-way hash of your license key — never the key itself — together with how often it was activated (with the device label and country of the last activation), whether it has been revoked, and which Creem subscription and order it belongs to. Creem tells our server about purchases, renewals, cancellations, refunds and disputes so that access follows your subscription.
  • Google Sheets export (Pro, optional). If you choose “Sheets”, Chrome asks for your permission and the extension creates a spreadsheet in your Google Drive. Data goes directly from your browser to Google; we never see it. The extension only has access to files it creates (drive.file scope).
  • Payments. Purchases are processed by Creem, our merchant of record. Creem collects your payment details, name, email and billing country under its own privacy policy. We receive order information (such as email, country and subscription status) to provide support.

What we don't do

No analytics or tracking in the extension, no advertising, no sale of personal data, and no access to your browsing history.

Permissions

The extension asks to read pages on all sites because you can use it on any website you choose, and because it must re-inject itself after a page loads during automatic pagination. It only acts on the tab you are working with, when you use it.

Your rights

You can delete all local data by removing the extension. For questions, access or deletion requests about order data, contact hello@ogerek.com. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK) you may have additional rights, which we will honour.

Changes

We will update this page if anything changes and adjust the date above.
