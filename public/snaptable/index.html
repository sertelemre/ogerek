Questions? Answers.

How do I activate Pro?

After paying you get a license key by email (it's also in the Creem customer portal). In SnapTable, click the plan badge at the top of the panel, paste the key and press Activate.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes — one license works on up to 3 Chrome profiles. Use “Deactivate on this device” in the plan dialog to move it.

How do I cancel?

Any time, from the Creem customer portal. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Do you see the data I scrape?

No. Detection, extraction and file exports all happen inside your browser. See the privacy policy.

Is scraping allowed?

SnapTable reads what your browser already shows you. You're responsible for following each website's terms and the law where you live — see our terms.
